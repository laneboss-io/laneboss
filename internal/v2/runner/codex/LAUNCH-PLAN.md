Capture disposition belongs to planner/helper stage 6, after argv construction.
A qualified explicit capture.refused may permit the isolated initial launch
under the contract's visible discard disposition. It provides no primary
bytes, session/limit fact or complete inventory. Capture consumers validate
binding/config/target/launch correlation, explicit allowed/approval/generation
metadata, the protected current D6 policy and the retained plan's policy snapshot
before returning the owning refusal. Sealed argv replay of a refused plan also
rechecks the protected current D6 policy (TestReplayCapturePolicy). Registered capture carries no refusal
metadata. A prior refused capture blocks Resume/Turn. The actual consumed D6
approval and release-time revocation checks remain publisher/store obligations.
Integrity/authentication/unknown ownership cannot become that fallback.
